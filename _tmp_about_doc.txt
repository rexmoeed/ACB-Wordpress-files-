Who We Are
For high-quality, custom-printed packaging, America Custom Boxes is your trusted partner. As a manufacturer — not just a reseller — we control the process from design and material selection through printing, quality assurance, and delivery. That means your artwork comes off the press the way you approved it, and your deadline is ours to protect.
Whatever you’re packaging — cupcakes, candles, supplements, apparel, or a subscription kit — our team builds custom retail packaging that protects your product and elevates your brand.

About Us FAQs
What does America Custom Boxes do?
We’re a custom packaging manufacturer. We design, print, and deliver custom boxes for food, cosmetics, retail, CBD, and e-commerce brands across the US.
Do you really not charge for setup?
Correct — die and plate setup is free, and 1-color printing is free too. Professional design support is also included at no cost.
What is your minimum order?
Our minimum is 100 boxes, so you can order what your brand actually needs rather than committing to huge runs.
Do you offer eco-friendly packaging?
Yes — biodegradable, recyclable, and sustainable materials are available across our product range.
