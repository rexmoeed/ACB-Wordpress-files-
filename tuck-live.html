The tuck box is the workhorse of the packaging world - the folding carton behind everything from cosmetics and supplements to snacks, electronics accessories, and retail products. Its tuck-in flaps make it fast to assemble and easy to close without tape, it ships flat to save on freight and storage, and it offers a clean, printable canvas on every panel. Simple, economical, and infinitely brandable, it's the format most products should start with.
America Custom Boxes manufactures custom tuck boxes in every style and size, engineered to your exact product and printed to retail-grade standards. Whether you need a quick reverse tuck end for a lightweight item, a sturdier auto-lock bottom for something with weight, or a premium tuck-top with soft-touch and foil, we build the right structure - with precision die-cutting and locked color that keep your brand consistent from first order to reorder. And because die-cutting and plate setup are free, specifying the exact carton you need never carries setup fees.
From a startup's first 100 cartons to a recurring enterprise run, our tuck boxes give an affordable, professional launch and a reliable, repeatable carton you can scale - backed by free design, free 3D mockups, and free U.S. shipping.

Key Benefits

Tuck boxes deliver the best balance of cost, speed, and brand impact in packaging:
Cost-effective - Efficient material use and simple construction keep unit costs low.
Fast, tool-free assembly - Tuck flaps close securely without tape or glue.
Ships & stores flat - Lower freight and warehouse footprint.
Fully brandable - Print every panel edge-to-edge, inside and out.
Versatile fit - Works across cosmetics, food, supplements, retail, and e-commerce.
No setup fees - Free die and plate setup means full customization without hidden costs.

Custom sizing - Built to your exact product for a snug, professional fit.
Multiple lock styles - Reverse, straight, auto-lock, and tuck-top for any weight.
Retail-grade print - Sharp, consistent, on-brand graphics.
Sustainable stock - Recyclable, recycled-content, and kraft options.
Premium-capable - Add foil, soft-touch, and embossing to elevate the same structure.
